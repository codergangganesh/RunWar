RunWar Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 23, 2026 · Last Updated: September 23, 2026
Google API & OAuth 2.0 User Data Compliance

Welcome to RunWar (accessible via https://run-war-chi.vercel.app). RunWar is an outdoor running and fitness tracking application created to help athletes, runners, and walkers record workout activities, analyze performance metrics, and synchronize historical fitness progress.

This Privacy Policy describes in complete detail how RunWar collects, uses, handles, stores, and protects user data, specifically including information obtained through Google OAuth 2.0 and Google Fitness APIs.

1. Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

Google Limited Use Compliance Statement

RunWar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will strictly adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • No Advertising: Google user data accessed via Google APIs is never used or transferred for serving advertisements, retargeting, or personalized marketing.
  • No Data Selling or Brokering: We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize Google user data to data brokers, advertising networks, or third parties under any circumstances.
  • No Human Viewing: No human is permitted to read or inspect your private Google fitness data unless you explicitly request customer support assistance or it is strictly necessary for security audits and technical diagnostics with your explicit authorization.
  • No AI Model Training: Google user data is not used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models without your affirmative opt-in consent.

2. Specific Google OAuth Scopes & Purpose of Access

RunWar requests access to the following Google OAuth 2.0 scopes with granular, read-only permissions:

OAuth Scope Data Accessed Specific Purpose in RunWar
.../auth/fitness.activity.read Completed workout sessions (runs, walks, jogs), active duration, step counts, and active calorie burn. To import past running sessions into your RunWar workout history, calculate total lifetime distance, and populate weekly activity progress charts.
.../auth/fitness.location.read GPS location samples, route trackpoints, and elevation profiles. To reconstruct interactive GPS route maps and calculate per-kilometer/mile pace splits for your imported runs.
.../auth/userinfo.email Google account email address. To display which Google account is currently linked in the in-app Connected Health settings.
.../auth/userinfo.profile / openid Basic profile identity. To authenticate the connection session securely.

3. Native Application Data Collection

In addition to connected Google Health data, RunWar collects the following information during native app usage:

  • Live GPS Location Data: Precise location coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude, timestamp, speed, and accuracy) are collected strictly in foreground while you actively track a workout. GPS tracking stops immediately when you pause, finish, or discard your run.
  • User Profile Settings: Name, optional body weight and height (used for calorie calculations), and preferred unit systems (Metric km vs Imperial miles).
  • Workout Logs: Total distance, duration, average speed, max speed, splits, and user-provided notes.

4. How We Process & Store Your Data

All data collected directly or imported from Google APIs is processed with industry-standard security protocols:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your device, RunWar, and Google APIs use TLS 1.3/HTTPS encryption.
  • Secure Cloud Storage: Data is persisted in our PostgreSQL backend platform protected with Row Level Security (RLS) policies. Each user's database records are segregated and accessible only through their verified user ID.
  • Deduplication Engine: RunWar employs automated timestamp and external ID matching to prevent duplicate counting between native GPS runs and imported Google Health workouts.

5. Data Retention, Revocation & Deletion

RunWar provides comprehensive self-service controls over your data sovereignty:

  • Disconnecting Google Account: You can disconnect Google Health at any time directly in the app via Profile > Connected Health > Disconnect, or by revoking access in your Google Account Security Settings. Revocation immediately terminates API access tokens.
  • Exporting Your Workouts: You can export your full workout history in standard CSV, GPX, or TCX formats via Profile > Export All Workouts.
  • Permanent Data Deletion: You can permanently delete your entire workout history and GPS logs at any time via Profile > Privacy & Data > Delete All Workout History. Upon confirmation, all associated records are permanently purged from database tables and local caches.

6. Third-Party Disclosures & Transfers

RunWar does not transfer or disclose your personal or fitness data to any third parties except:

  • Backend Infrastructure: Secure cloud database and hosting providers (InsForge and Vercel) strictly under confidentiality and data processing agreements for hosting operations.
  • Legal Compliance: When strictly required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

7. Children's Privacy

RunWar is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal or location data from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us for immediate deletion.

8. Updates to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product enhancements, API updates, or legal compliance. Any material changes will be published on this page with an updated Effective Date.
